## Report

### Time is rushing

Twenty years ago I was sitting in the bus crossing the path of the famous West Highland Way (WHW)
and my mum said that one day it would be great to walk it through. Since that time the north part of
it alone in 2018, and five years later, Bee and I hiked in the reverse direction from the Fort
Williams at the north to the Milgavie in the south in six days. Each of these experiences was
totally different due to weather and other circumstances and every time it was absolutely great.
Uniqueness of this long distance way is in combining several very different landscapes containing
village and suburb sceneries, historical sights of historical battles and dismantled monasteries
with highland sceneries and loch lomond and Trosach natural park. Every day gives a new perspective
like you would walk six different tracks combined in one short.
